About Me

Business Intelligence, ETL and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and data analytics within freight forwarding and logistics.

I enjoy working out how data is held inside business applications, turning it into a clear reporting structure and using it to answer practical questions about performance, cost and capacity.

From Applications to Reporting

My work starts with the source systems. CargoWise is the main application I work with, alongside experience with WebCargo, MaxOptra, PeopleHR, Shopify and Jira. SharePoint, Viva Insights and analytics platforms are supporting sources within that wider application and reporting experience.

A large operational database can contain thousands of tables and is not automatically ready for reporting. I use SQL to extract and transform the required data at source, load it through Microsoft Fabric, build cleaner Lakehouse tables and semantic models, and then produce Power BI reports from that reporting-ready data.

CargoWise source database→SQL & ETL at source→Fabric Lakehouse→Clean models→Power BI reports

The source remains a large operational structure. Fabric holds the selected reporting data in a cleaner form, giving the business more control over how it is stored, transformed, modelled and reused across reports.

My Background

My interest in technology started with a Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship before I progressed to a Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

That applications support background still shapes how I work. I understand the operational process first, then use ETL, data modelling and reporting to make the underlying data more useful.

Microsoft Fabric & BI

I have worked with Microsoft Fabric since its early days and have used it for data ingestion, pipelines, Lakehouse storage, notebooks, transformations, semantic models and Power BI reporting.

I am a Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) and a CargoWise Certified Professional. Together, these reflect both sides of my work: understanding business applications and building the data platform and reports around them.
