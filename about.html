About Me

Business Intelligence, Analytics and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in Business Intelligence, reporting and data analytics within the freight forwarding and logistics industry.

I enjoy using data to understand how a business is performing and turning that data into reporting that can support better decisions.

My Background

My passion for technology started through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship before progressing to a Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

Since then, I've developed my skills across SQL, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, combining my technical experience with the operational knowledge I've gained from working within freight forwarding and logistics.

Microsoft Fabric & BI

I've worked with Microsoft Fabric since its early days and have enjoyed seeing the platform develop while using it to build my own knowledge and experience.

I recently achieved the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) certification and I am also a CargoWise Certified Professional, combining my BI skills with knowledge of the industry I work in.

Outside of Work

Outside of work, I enjoy staying active through things such as running, the gym, hiking and padel. I've completed multiple half marathons and I'm currently preparing for my first marathon next year.

I'm always looking for the next challenge, whether that's developing my skills professionally or pushing myself outside of work.
