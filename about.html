About Me

An applications support background developed into a career in data and business intelligence

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and data analytics within freight forwarding and logistics.

I enjoy understanding how data sits behind business applications, turning it into a clear reporting structure and using it to answer real business questions and support better decision making.

From Applications to Reporting

My work starts with business applications. CargoWise is the main application I work with, alongside WebCargo, MaxOptra, PeopleHR, Shopify and Jira. I also work with other systems and data sources such as SharePoint, Viva Insights and analytics platforms, using their data in ETL processes and reporting.

One of the main things I have worked on is building a central reporting data source in Microsoft Fabric. A large part of the data comes from CargoWise, where I use SQL to extract and transform data from across the system for reporting. I also bring together data from other business applications and sources using different ETL and ingestion methods. The aim has been to create one central place where reporting data can be prepared, structured and modelled properly, rather than treating every Power BI report as a separate piece of work. This creates consistent, reliable data and reusable models that can support reporting across different areas of the business.

Data source→SQL & ETL→Fabric Lakehouse→Semantic models→Power BI reports

My Background

My interest in technology started with a Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship before I progressed to a Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

That applications support background still shapes how I work. I understand the business and operational processes first, then use ETL, data modelling and reporting to turn the data within those applications into reliable information that supports better decision making.

Microsoft Fabric & BI

I've worked with Microsoft Fabric since its early days, developing my knowledge and experience alongside the platform as it has continued to grow.

I am a Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) and a CargoWise Certified Professional, combining my experience in BI, data and business applications with knowledge of the freight forwarding and logistics industry.
