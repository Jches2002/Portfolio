Who I am

Business intelligence with an operational background

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and analytics within freight forwarding and logistics.

My career began through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician Apprenticeship before progressing into application support and a Level 4 Data Analyst Apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

Today I design and develop reporting solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, SQL and DAX. My support background helps me understand the systems, processes and people behind the data, not just the final dashboard.

I hold the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate and CargoWise Certified Professional certifications.

Outside work, I enjoy running, the gym, hiking and padel.
