About Me

Business Intelligence, Analytics and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and analytics within the freight forwarding and logistics industry.

My career began through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician Apprenticeship before progressing into application support and then a Level 4 Data Analyst Apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

Today I design and develop reporting solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, SQL and DAX. My application support background helps me understand the systems, operational processes and people behind the data, not just the final dashboard.

I hold the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate certification and the CargoWise Certified Professional certification. I am continuing to develop my skills in analytics engineering and modern data platforms.

Outside of work, I enjoy running, going to the gym, hiking and playing padel. I have completed half marathons and I am working towards a marathon next year.
