About Me

Business Intelligence, Analytics and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and analytics within the freight forwarding and logistics industry.

My career began through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician Apprenticeship before progressing into application support and then a Level 4 Data Analyst Apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

Today I design and develop reporting solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, SQL and DAX. My support background helps me understand the systems, operational processes and people behind the data, not just the final dashboard.

I hold the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate certification and the CargoWise Certified Professional certification.

Outside work, I enjoy running, the gym, hiking and padel.
