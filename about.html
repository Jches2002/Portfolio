Business Intelligence, Analytics and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am


I am an Application Support Analyst specialising in Business Intelligence, reporting and analytics within the freight forwarding and logistics industry.


My career began through a Level 3 Digital Support Technician Apprenticeship before progressing to a Level 4 Data Analyst Apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.


Today I design and develop reporting solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI and SQL, helping transform business data into meaningful insights and decision-making tools.


I hold the Microsoft Certified Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) certification and continue to develop my skills in analytics engineering and modern data platforms.
