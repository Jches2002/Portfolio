About Me

Business Intelligence, ETL and Microsoft Fabric

Who I Am

I am an Application Support Analyst - BI specialising in business intelligence, reporting and data analytics within freight forwarding and logistics.

I enjoy working out how data is held inside business applications, turning it into a clear reporting structure and using it to answer practical questions about performance, cost and capacity.

From Applications to Reporting

My work starts with business applications. CargoWise is the main application I work with, alongside WebCargo, MaxOptra, PeopleHR, Shopify and Jira. I also work with other systems and data sources such as SharePoint, Viva Insights and analytics platforms, using their data in ETL processes and reporting.

The CargoWise source is a large, complex database and the data is not automatically ready for reporting. I extract and transform the required data at source using SQL, normalising or denormalising it where needed. Microsoft Fabric then ingests the required data into a clean Lakehouse, where further transformation and cleaning prepares it for semantic models and reusable, report-ready data.

CargoWise source database→SQL & ETL at source→Fabric Lakehouse→Clean models→Power BI reports

My Background

My interest in technology started with a Level 3 Digital Support Technician apprenticeship before I progressed to a Level 4 Data Analyst apprenticeship, where I achieved a Distinction.

That applications support background still shapes how I work. I understand the operational process first, then use ETL, data modelling and reporting to make the data within those applications more useful.

Microsoft Fabric & BI

I've worked with Microsoft Fabric since its early days and have enjoyed seeing the platform develop while using it to build my own knowledge and experience.

I am a Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) and a CargoWise Certified Professional, combining my BI skills with knowledge of the industry I work in.
